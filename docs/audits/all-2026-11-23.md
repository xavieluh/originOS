## 2. Findings and what was done

No BLOCKER was found.

### Security, MAJOR

1. **GitHub webhook replay under a new delivery id.**
   - **Finding:** GitHub signs only the body, and the `X-GitHub-Delivery` header is unsigned. A captured body re-posted with a new delivery id was accepted (`packages/connect/src/webhooks.ts`, `core.accept_webhook`).
   - **Fixed:** migration 0012 answers `duplicate` when the connection has already recorded that body (same SHA-256). An index supporting this is added in 0013. Tested in `packages/db/test/integration/connect.test.ts`.
   - **Remaining:** a captured body can still be posted once to another connection of the same provider. Per-connection secrets must come before Phase 21 consumes deliveries; this is recorded in ADR-027 and THREAT_MODEL.
2. **Canonical tables trusted the application role.**
   - **Finding:** any member could approve an agent action as anyone, or un-revoke an identity claim (`0005` policies with no guard triggers).
   - **Fixed (0012):**
     - An approval starts pending and is decided once, by a person acting as themselves (never as an agent), who is not the subject that asked.
     - A tool action is approved only with an approved approval, and a finished action keeps its outcome.
     - Revoking or superseding a claim is one-way, and only owners, admins or the claim's subject can do it.
   - **Tests:** `packages/db/test/integration/guards.test.ts`.

### Security, MINOR

3. **An agent subject could be created in another organization of the same owner.** Fixed: `core.owns_agent` now requires the agent to be in the current organization (0012, tested).
4. **Account proofs were trusted from the application role alone.**
   - Fixed: a verified account proof needs a consumed identity-proof sign-in by the same person, for that profile, within 10 minutes (0012, tested).
   - Remaining: the guard does not bind the exact account the provider returned (ADR-028, Known limits).
5. **Owners and admins could edit a member's creator profile and proof visibility at the database level.** Fixed: they moderate but cannot change content or what proofs show (0012, tested).
6. **An admin with a second account could take over a member's profile.** Not fixed. The approval is recorded and the member's proofs end, but the member is not notified and nobody outside the organization reviews it. Owner notification is an optional idea that has not been approved. Platform review comes in Phase 41. Recorded in ADR-028.
7. **The provider login appeared in the `integration.connected` audit metadata.** Fixed: removed (`connect.service.ts`).
8. **Global handles reveal whether a handle exists.** Accepted and documented, because handles are public names (ADR-028).
9. **Public pages showed the detail of a failed re-check,** which can describe the owner's network. Fixed: they now show "The re-check on <date> failed." (0012, tested).
