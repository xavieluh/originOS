### Required engineering deliverables
1. Implement policy engine for action type, external domain, sensitivity, budget and role.
2. support pending approvals with expiry, double-confirmation and immutable decision events.
3. enforce only for tool calls routed through our gateway or supported hooks.
4. generate signed execution receipts linking inputs, observed events, policy decisions, approval and output hashes.
5. include emergency stop on managed flows.
